A final decision can be buried between dozens of messages and later changes. An MCP Client can examine observed Conversation Activity around a topic, distinguish proposals from agreement, and show the context you need to validate its answer.

## Look for changes, not just keywords

Name the topic, likely people or groups, and a time range broad enough to include revisions. Ask for the latest observed decision plus earlier options that materially changed.

## Try asking

> What did we finally decide about the venue?

> Did the delivery date change after Tuesday?

> Show the context around the pricing decision.

## Treat history honestly

Observed history is not provider certified complete. Known Ingestion Gaps are disclosed. If WhatsApp reports a message deletion, a Deleted Message Tombstone prevents a late provider delivery from restoring that content.
