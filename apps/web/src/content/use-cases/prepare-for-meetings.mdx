The useful context for a meeting often arrives in scattered messages. With explicit read permission, an MCP Client can gather the observed WhatsApp context around the people and topic, then turn it into a brief you can verify.

## Build a practical meeting brief

Ask for recent decisions, commitments by person, open questions, and changes since the last meeting. Keep uncertain statements separate from confirmed agreements.

## Try asking

> Prepare me for the launch meeting using this week’s WhatsApp context.

> What did Ana and I agree before today’s call?

> List the open decisions for the supplier meeting.

## Verify what matters

Ask the MCP Client to include the relevant source context for dates, prices, and commitments. Normal describes message history as observed rather than provider certified complete, and discloses known Ingestion Gaps when applicable.
