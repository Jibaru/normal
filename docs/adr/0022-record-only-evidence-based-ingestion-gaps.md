# Record only evidence-based ingestion gaps

Maintain connection-scoped Ingestion Gap intervals from concrete evidence, not message inactivity: provider disconnection or webhook-configuration drift found by five-minute reconciliation, a measured lapse of more than two reconciliation intervals, verified deliveries that fail durable enqueue or bounded normalization and reach DLQ, measured ingress or Queue outages and purges, and data loss during restore. A reconciliation lapse is recorded as `health_check_failure` from the last completed observation through the next completed observation, even when that recovery observation is healthy. Other intervals begin conservatively at the last confirmed healthy point and close only on confirmed recovery where applicable. Gaps remain visible until they age out with the affected Message History Window because Wasender offers no backfill. Message reads return intersecting causes and call history observed rather than complete; silent provider omission while every available signal stays healthy is acknowledged as undetectable.
