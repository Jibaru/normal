Follow ups rarely arrive in a tidy task list. They appear as questions, promises, introductions, and dates inside ongoing chats. Normal lets your chosen MCP Client review relevant observed messages and extract candidate next steps.

## Define what counts as a follow up

Set a date range and ask for categories such as promises you made, questions awaiting your answer, or introductions you offered. Review the result before turning anything into a real task.

## Try asking

> What follow ups did I commit to this week?

> Which questions have I not answered since Monday?

> Find introductions I said I would make.

## Keep the result grounded

Ask for the conversation and date behind every candidate item. Availability follows the WhatsApp Connection’s Message Retention Policy, which defaults to 30 days unless you choose another supported period.
