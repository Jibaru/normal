Fast moving groups mix plans, reactions, side conversations, and decisions. Normal gives an approved MCP Client access to observed group messages so it can organize the useful parts around your question.

## Ask for the signal you need

The best summary is specific about the group, time window, and desired result.

* Decisions separated from casual discussion
* Unanswered questions with the surrounding context
* A concise timeline of meaningful changes

## Try asking

> Summarize what the family group decided about Saturday.

> Give me the unresolved questions from the event group.

## What counts as activity?

Conversation Activity is the latest inbound or outbound Stored Message. Directory changes, receipts, and reactions do not count as Conversation Activity.

Every MCP Authorization applies only to the WhatsApp Connections you select, and a connection added later does not enter the grant automatically.
