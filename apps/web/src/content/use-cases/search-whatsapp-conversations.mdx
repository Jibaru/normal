WhatsApp search works best when you know the exact phrase. An MCP Client can instead search approved WhatsApp context by person, group, topic, or approximate date.

## Search with the context you remember

You can combine partial clues such as a person, a month, and a topic. Ask for the supporting conversation context so you can verify the result instead of receiving an isolated answer.

* Find an address without remembering who sent it
* Recover a recommendation from a busy group
* Locate when a deadline or decision changed

## Try asking

> What hotel did Valeria recommend for Cusco?

> When did we agree to move the launch date?

## History has a clear boundary

The Message History Window begins no earlier than Connection Setup. Reads also disclose a known Ingestion Gap when it intersects the history you requested. No known gap does not prove that the provider delivered every message.
