Client work often lives across formal systems and informal WhatsApp messages. An MCP Client can review approved conversation context, surface unanswered requests, and prepare a response without automatically taking action.

## Review the conversation by category

Ask for client requests, your commitments, deadlines, and uncertain items in separate sections. This makes it easier to verify the result and decide what actually needs a response.

## Try asking

> Which client questions are still unanswered this week?

> What did I promise the Acme group by Friday?

> Draft a status update from our latest conversation.

## Access remains personal

Normal’s ownership boundary is one User and one Personal Account, not an organization or team. You can revoke an MCP Client’s authorization without deleting the underlying WhatsApp Connection.
